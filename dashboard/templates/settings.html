About Sentinel-LEWS

Sentinel-LEWS (Landslide Early Warning System) is an offline, edge-native AI system designed for district-level disaster management operations.

The system operates independently during network failures, providing continuous risk assessment and alert capabilities through local inference and GSM-based communication.
